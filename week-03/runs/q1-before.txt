ANSWER
The fee differs by version of the fee schedule:

- Version 8 (current): the overdraft fee is $29 per item paid, and the returned item fee is $29 per item returned, with no more than three such fees charged to one account per business day; no overdraft fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by $10 or less at the end of the business day [consumer-fee-schedule.html].
- Version 7: the overdraft fee is $35 per item paid, and the returned item fee is $35 per item returned, with no more than four such fees charged to one account per business day [consumer-fee-schedule-v7.html].

In both versions, an overdraft transfer from a linked savings account is free [consumer-fee-schedule.html][consumer-fee-schedule-v7.html].

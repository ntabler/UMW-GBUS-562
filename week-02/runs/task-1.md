### You

You are a marketing analyst for Wildframe Coffee Roasters.

Wildframe Coffee Roasters is a specialty coffee roaster that sells beans online and through a monthly subscription. Customers are environmentally conscious urban professionals. Brand voice is warm, plainspoken, and a little irreverent, with no corporate jargon.

Wildframe wants new perks for subscribers who stay six months or longer. Get 6 perk ideas. 

The response must be  a numbered list of exactly 6 ideas, each one sentence of no more than 25 words.

### Claude

1. Free bag of our rarest single-origin beans, because loyalty deserves something you can't just buy off the shelf.
2. A permanent 10% discount, no coupon codes or hoops—just cheaper coffee forever.
3. Early dibs on seasonal roasts before they hit the website and sell out in minutes.
4. A handwritten thank-you note from our roaster, because robots don't appreciate loyalty like we do.
5. A shipping upgrade so your coffee arrives faster instead of sitting sad in a truck.

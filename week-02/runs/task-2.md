### You

You are a marketing analyst for Wildframe Coffee Roasters.

Wildframe Coffee Roasters is a specialty coffee roaster that sells beans online and through a monthly subscription. Customers are environmentally conscious urban professionals. 

Wildframe is adding three coffees to its shop, and every coffee gets a bean card. Respond with one card for each coffee below. The response must be three cards in the house format, with the same labels in the same order.

Coffee:

- Cerrado, Brazil. Medium-dark roast. Notes: peanut, milk chocolate, low acidity. Good for espresso or French press.
- Chiapas, Mexico. Medium roast. Notes: almond, caramel, orange peel. Good for drip or AeroPress.

Example of house bean card format:
HUILA, COLOMBIA
